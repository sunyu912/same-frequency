01起点Premise

不是“多陪陪老人”，
而是换一种方式听见彼此。 Not “spend more time with elders” — but a different way of hearing each other.

常规公益叙事The usual story

老人孤独，所以年轻人应该多陪伴、多聊天。Older people are lonely, so young people should visit and talk more.

默认“更多交流 = 更多理解”，默认所有人都想用同一种方式被陪伴。It assumes more talk means more understanding — and that everyone wants to be kept company in the same way.

同频的提问What Same Frequency asks

不同年龄、性格、沟通与认知方式的人，对“被理解”的需求是否不同？Do people of different ages, temperaments and ways of thinking need different things in order to feel understood?

亲缘关系、交流频率或观点一致，并不自动等于理解。有时，放慢节奏、等待、允许沉默，反而让人更容易被听见。Being family, talking often or agreeing doesn’t automatically mean being understood. Sometimes slowing down, waiting and letting silence be makes it easier to be heard.

核心研究问题Core research question

有意义的代际连接，需要的是
“更多交流”，还是“更匹配的交流方式”？ Does meaningful connection across generations need more communication — or better-matched communication?

距离，可以是一种尊重。Distance can be respectful.

沉默，也可以是交流。Silence can be communication.

耐心，本身就是一种理解。Patience can be a form of understanding.

— 同频项目的三个信念— Three beliefs behind Same Frequency
● 330 Hz  +  ● 220 Hz  =  3 : 2

两个不同的频率，不必变成同一个，也能组成稳定、和谐的图形。Two different frequencies don’t have to become one to form a steady, harmonious shape.

02沟通频谱The spectrum

“合不来”，
往往只是频率没对上。Not getting along is often just a mismatch in frequency.

03研究方法Method

从真实的故事出发，
而不是预设的答案。Starting from real stories, not presumed answers.

访谈、问卷、数据分析、设计、测试、公开表达：六个阶段，把一个人的观察变成可验证、可设计、可实践的探索。Interviews, a survey, analysis, design, testing, then sharing the work. Six phases turn a personal observation into something that can be tested, designed and put into practice.

04四种交流实验Four formats

不强迫所有人
都用“聊天”来连接。Nobody has to connect by talking.

根据数据设计四种低压力的交流方式，让双方自己选择。每次体验后，都用一份简短反馈记录：舒不舒服、节奏是否被尊重、是否感到被理解。Four low-pressure formats designed from the data, and each pair picks their own. After every session a short check-in asks how comfortable it felt, whether each person’s pace was respected, and whether they felt understood.

07伦理边界Ethics

尊重，
先于一切数据。Respect comes before any data.

这是一个关于理解的项目，所以它首先必须是一个尊重人的项目。A project about understanding people has to start by respecting them.

不贴标签No labels

不把安静、慢回应、重复表达等行为自动解释为神经多样性；只有本人或家庭主动提供时才记录相关背景。Quietness, slow replies or repetition are never read as signs of neurodivergence. Such background is recorded only when a participant or their family offers it.

不追问诊断No diagnosis-seeking

医疗或认知信息不是项目需要的数据，我们不会为此主动询问。Medical or cognitive information isn’t data this project needs, and we won’t ask for it.

随时跳过，随时退出Skip or leave at any time

任何参与者都可以跳过任何问题，或在任何时候退出。Anyone can skip any question or stop taking part at any point.

匿名与同意Anonymity and consent

记录只使用匿名编号（如 Y01、O01）；未经同意，不录音，不公开声音、照片、故事或原话。Records use anonymous IDs such as Y01 and O01. Nothing is recorded, and no voice, photo, story or quote is published, without consent.

适当的同意流程Proper consent pathways

面对可能存在认知能力受损的长辈，不由学生自行判断其能否同意，而是通过家属、机构或指导老师的适当流程。For older adults who may have cognitive impairment, the student never decides alone whether they can consent. Family members, care organisations or a supervising teacher handle that decision.

不做治疗声明No treatment claims

本项目不是医学诊断或治疗研究，不声称改善、治疗或干预自闭症、失智症等状况。This is not medical or clinical research. It makes no claim to improve, treat or intervene in autism, dementia or any other condition.

先确认，再升级Review before scaling up

若未来升级为正式发表、竞赛或导师指导的人体研究，将先由学校 / 导师确认伦理与知情同意要求。If the work grows into formal publication, a competition or supervised human-subjects research, a school or mentor will review the ethics and consent requirements first.

09怎样才算成功What success means

不是“让老人更开心”，
或“让两代人更亲近”。Not “making elders happier” or “bringing generations closer.”

而是证明：
“一种沟通方式适合所有人”
并不成立。But showing why “one way of communicating fits everyone” doesn’t hold.

  • 01识别至少 3–5 种真实存在的 communication mismatch。Identify at least 3–5 real communication mismatches.
  • 02用访谈与数据，解释为什么没有万能的沟通方式。Use interviews and data to explain why no single way of communicating works for everyone.
  • 03根据参与者反馈，迭代至少一种交流形式。Revise at least one format based on participant feedback.
  • 04最终输出既有数据，也有人真实的声音与选择。End with both data and people’s own voices and choices.
  • 05诚实说明：哪些看法改变了，哪些仍然保留。Say honestly which views changed and which remain.
